KGP Bazaar ／ Redesign v2

Warm Editorial Marketplace

A campus classifieds board that reads like a printed zine, not a SaaS dashboard. Warm paper ground, oxblood brand ink, one amber accent, an expressive serif reserved for numbers and titles. Everything else is quiet so the product photos and prices do the talking.

Core principle — the old design had four competing colors on every card and a green button repeated 20 times down the page. Here color is a scarce resource: maroon = brand/you, amber = wanted/negotiable, green = only the single WhatsApp action you're about to take.

Notes
Bottom nav is now 5 slots — Browse, Wanted, ＋, Saved, You. Wanted stops being a hidden mode toggle and Saved gets out of the profile page where nobody finds it.
4:5 portrait media. Students shoot items vertically on a phone. The current 4:3 crop chops the top and bottom off almost every upload.
Price is the hero. Set in Instrument Serif at 25px in ink, not as a colored badge. It reads as a number, scans fast, and stops fighting the condition pills for attention.
WhatsApp as an icon, not a wall. A 31px green button sits beside the save heart — still one tap to contact, but a dot of green per card instead of a full-width bar ×20. On desktop, hovering the card expands it to show the word.
Sticky header collapses. Scrolling down hides search and the rail, leaving a 48px bar with the wordmark and a search icon. Scrolling up brings it back.

Feed — desktop

Not a stretched phone. Search moves into the header, an editorial masthead gives the page a voice, and the grid runs 4-up on a single 1280px container.
